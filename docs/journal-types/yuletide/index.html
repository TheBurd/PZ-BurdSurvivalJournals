Yuletide journals

Yuletide journals are the dev branch's seasonal gift family: wrapped journal variants that can replace worn or bloody rolls, grant bundled supplies when unwrapped, and even show up as safehouse deliveries during the active season.

What it is

The dev UI strings define Yuletide journals as a wrapped seasonal gift journal with its own badge, flavor text, unwrap prompt, reveal flow, and bundled-supplies reward lane. They are not just reskinned worn journals.

How the season works

  • Enable Yuletide Journals turns the seasonal lane on.
  • The season can activate from the in-game December calendar, the host's real-world date, or both.
  • When active, eligible worn and bloody rolls can be replaced by wrapped Yuletide journals.
  • Cursed journals can optionally switch to Krampus-authored seasonal identity during the same period.

What happens when you unwrap one

The context action is explicitly Unwrap Gift Journal... rather than a normal open prompt. Unwrapping reveals the journal, grants any bundled supplies, and consumes the one-time gift layer of the item.

One-time gift reveal. The dev UI tooltip explicitly notes that bundled gifts are granted once when the wrapping comes off.

Gift tier odds and bundle rules

Tier Default weight Bundle behavior
Practical 70 Common survival supplies.
Rare 25 Weapons, ammo, and stronger utility gear.
Jackpot 5 Heavy-hitter items like generators, sledgehammers, and high-end weapons.
  • Each unwrap rolls one tier, then takes 1 to 2 distinct gift entries from that tier by default.
  • The pool is shuffled before selection, so a single unwrap does not duplicate the same entry type unless that entry's own count roll is above 1.
  • If the stored gift list is empty for some reason, the server rerolls a fresh bundle on unwrap.

Key sandbox controls

  • Enable Yuletide Journals governs whether the seasonal replacement lane exists at all.
  • Yuletide Worn Replacement and Yuletide Bloody Replacement control how often the seasonal swap happens in world loot and corpse drops.
  • Yuletide Min/Max Skills, Min/Max XP, and the trait/recipe/forget-slot toggles control the journal rewards after unwrap.
  • Yuletide Gift Practical/Rare/Jackpot Weight plus the min/max item counts control the physical bundled gift pool.
  • Krampus Seasonal Cursed Authors lets active Yuletide seasons re-skin newly spawned cursed journals around Krampus instead of a random survivor identity.

Trivia

Yuletide journal notes

  • The dev strings call the rarity label Seasonal Gift, which gives Yuletide journals a distinct identity instead of treating them as plain recolors.
  • They are one of the only journal families with an explicit unwrap sequence, reveal copy, and bundled-item lead text baked directly into the UI flow.
  • The seasonal system is broad enough to affect worn journals, bloody journals, cursed journal author identity, and safehouse delivery behavior at the same time.
  • The default tier weights are intentionally lopsided, so most bundles stay practical while a small jackpot lane still leaves room for memorable holiday spikes.
