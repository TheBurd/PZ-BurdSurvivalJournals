The current mod surface is not just clean, worn, and bloody anymore. Use this page as the taxonomy hub, then move into the dedicated dossiers for the full behavior, tuning notes, and trivia on each family.

Personal Journals

Clean and restored journals

RecordableCan be persistentSupports multiple reward tabs

These are the player-authored progression journals. They exist to capture earned progress and let it be reclaimed later under the server's chosen recovery rules.

  • Can support skills, traits, recipes, and stats.
  • May carry restored or persistent state badges.
  • May either persist forever or dissolve after use depending on how restored journal behavior is configured.

Yuletide journals

Seasonal replacementBundled suppliesWrapped and unwrapped states

Yuletide journals replace some worn and bloody journal rolls during the active season, can deliver bundled gifts on unwrap, and can even show up as safehouse deliveries during the same seasonal window.
