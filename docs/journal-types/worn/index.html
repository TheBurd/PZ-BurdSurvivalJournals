Worn journals

Worn journals are the quieter side of the loot-journal ecosystem: container finds, survivor leftovers, and low-profile reward sources that can still carry far more than plain skill XP.

What it is

Worn journals are world-found survivor notes. They fill the role of exploratory loot rather than player-authored storage, which makes them a good early reference point for new players learning how BSJ feels without touching the full recovery system yet.

What can roll on them

  • Skill entries with configurable minimum and maximum counts.
  • Recipe rewards if worn recipes are enabled and the recipe chance succeeds.
  • Trait rewards if worn traits are enabled and the trait chance succeeds.
  • A forget slot if that optional reward lane is enabled.

Why they matter

The current sandbox options make worn journals much more flexible than their older reputation. They can stay close to the classic lightweight skill-note model, or they can become mini jackpot containers with multiple reward tabs.

Key sandbox controls

  • Enable Worn Journal Spawns and Worn Spawn Chance % control whether they appear at all.
  • Worn Min/Max Skills and Worn Min/Max XP define the basic skill lane.
  • Enable Worn Journal Recipes and Worn Recipe Chance % turn recipe rewards on or off.
  • Enable Worn Journal Traits and Enable Worn Forget Slot decide whether they can cross into higher-impact progression rewards.

Trivia

Worn journal notes

  • Worn journals are the family most players still mentally file under the old "skills only" behavior, even though the sandbox now supports much more.
  • The worn family is the clearest example of BSJ hiding disabled tabs while preserving existing journal data for later re-enabling.
  • Because worn journals come from containers rather than corpses, they tend to feel like environmental storytelling instead of combat payoff.
