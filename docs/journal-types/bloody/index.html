Bloody journals

Bloody journals are the heavier corpse-loot branch of the system. They sit above worn journals in reward pressure and are designed to feel like standout finds rather than background filler.

What it is

Bloody journals are the corpse-loot upgrade over worn journals. They are the family most likely to feel like a real progression hit after combat, because both the spawn lane and the reward pool are tuned to be stronger.

What makes them stronger

  • Higher minimum and maximum XP per skill than worn journals.
  • Trait and recipe rolls tuned as a higher-value lane.
  • Optional passive-skill support for Strength and Fitness if loot passive skills are enabled.
  • Optional interaction with skill book multipliers on loot-journal absorption.

Key sandbox controls

  • Enable Bloody Journal Drops and Bloody Drop Chance % control how often zombies can drop them.
  • Bloody Min/Max Skills and Bloody Min/Max XP define the basic reward range.
  • Enable Bloody Journal Traits and Rare Trait Chance % control the trait lane.
  • Enable Bloody Journal Recipes and Enable Bloody Forget Slot let them branch into higher-complexity rewards.

How they fit with other systems

Bloody journals benefit from loot-journal-specific tuning. That means they are the family most likely to be affected by optional skill book multiplier support and by any server rules that limit how many successful claims a loot journal can survive before dissolving.

Trivia

Bloody journal notes

  • Bloody journals are the most direct "combat paid off" journal family in BSJ.
  • They are also the clearest place where the mod's higher-value loot tuning shows up without involving cursed penalties.
  • If you want BSJ loot to feel exciting without adding risk gimmicks, bloody journals are usually the lane that gets pushed first.
