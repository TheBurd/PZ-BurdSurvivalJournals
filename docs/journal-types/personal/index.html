What it is

Personal journals are the clean, player-managed side of the system. They are the only family built around long-term ownership, repeat access, and server-controlled recovery rules instead of random loot generation.

When the right tabs are enabled, they can record skills, traits, recipes, and player stats such as kills or hours survived.

Why players use them

  • Recover earned progress after death or character turnover.
  • Build a controlled progression backup rather than relying only on world loot.
  • Track more than skills once the trait, recipe, and stat tabs are enabled.

How the state system works

Personal journals can present as persistent or restored. The UI strings explicitly separate these states, because restored journals can keep their loot-origin identity even after becoming player-owned.

Persistent

Reusable recovery journal behavior with no auto-dissolve after claims.

Restored

Salvaged journal behavior. Depending on the server, it may dissolve after all claims or behave like a reusable persistent journal.

Key sandbox controls

  • Only Record Earned Progress blocks profession starting bonuses and similar baseline data from being recorded as free recovery value.
  • Enable Trait Recording (Player), Enable Recipe Recording (Player), and Enable Stat Recording decide which tabs exist.
  • Allow Others to Open and Allow Others to Claim control multiplayer sharing behavior.
  • Allow Restored Journal Dissolution decides how restored journals behave after their rewards are fully claimed.

Trivia

Personal journal notes

  • Personal journals are the only family that clearly centers authorship and permission rules instead of random discovery.
  • The restored badge exists because not every player-owned journal starts life as a pristine crafted journal.
  • BSJ's baseline restriction turns personal journals into an anti-exploit tool as much as a recovery system.
