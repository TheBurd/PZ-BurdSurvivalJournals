Cursed journals

Cursed journals are dormant hazard items first and reward sources second. The first player to break the seal takes the consequence, after which the journal converts into a stronger reward state.

What it is

Cursed journals are deliberately split into two phases: a dormant item state and an unleashed reward state. The first opener triggers one curse effect immediately, then the item converts into a buffed reward journal with its own skill, trait, recipe, and forget-slot profile.

How the seal break works

By default the server shuffles the whole curse handler list and takes the first valid result for the current opener. If a forced debug effect fails to find a valid target, the code falls back to the panic ambush instead of leaving the journal inert.

Always noisy. Breaking the seal always emits a world sound at radius 90 with volume 90, even if the actual curse rolled is not the panic ambush.

All curse effects

Effect What it does Live numbers
Barbed seal Inflicts an immediate hand wound on a random left or right hand. Sets a cut or scratch, forces bleeding time to at least 8, and forces additional pain to at least 12.
Jammed breath Hits endurance and layers panic plus stress on top. Endurance drops by 30% to 40%, panic rises by 18 to 30, and stress rises by 0.10 to 0.20.
Hexed tooling Damages one eligible weapon or tool from your equipped gear or inventory. Condition loss is 15% to 35% of the item's max durability, but it will not reduce the target below 1 condition.
Torn gear Rakes across currently worn clothing and adds fresh holes. Targets 3 to 5 new tears if eligible clothing coverage exists.
Seasonal wave Builds a heat-wave or cold-snap effect from the current world month. Warm months are March through August. Temperature shifts by about 4.5 to 7.0; warm rolls add 30 to 60 wetness, while cold rolls push cold strength to at least 70 to 100.
Pantsed Removes qualifying worn lower-body clothing. The handler removes every valid pantsed target it finds, not just a single piece.
Gain negative trait Adds one eligible drawback from the mod's removable-trait pool. Trait-conflict cleanup runs first, so mutually exclusive traits can be cancelled before the curse lands.
Lose positive trait Strips one currently owned positive trait. The handler only selects traits the opener actually has at the moment the seal breaks.
Lose skill level Pushes one learned skill back below its previous level threshold. The opener loses 1 current skill level from a valid skill with XP re-synced afterward.
Panic ambush Maxes panic and tries to turn the seal break into a mini horde event. Panic is set to 100 and the ambush tries to spawn 8 to 12 zombies.

If the server is configured to disguise cursed journals as bloody, the hazard still uses this same handler list after the reveal.

Panic ambush profile

Stage Live behavior
Spawn ring Fresh zombies are rolled in a ring 9 to 18 tiles away from the opener on the same Z level.
Spawn count The handler attempts 8 to 12 zombies, stopping early if it runs out of valid squares.
Immediate AI pull The panic event applies an ambush pull radius of 55 with pull volume 75.
Zombie nudges Nearby zombies in a 65-tile nudge radius are repeatedly repointed toward the player.
Delayed pulses Extra pull pulses fire at roughly 650 ms, 1250 ms, and 1850 ms, with delayed nudges at roughly 900 ms and 1700 ms.

That means the cursed shriek players notice is larger than the actual ambush pull ring: the seal-break sound reaches 90 tiles, while the panic handler's follow-up pull works inside the smaller 55/65-tile profile.

Why players still open them

The reward phase is configured separately from normal bloody journals and is intended to be richer. In practice, cursed journals turn journal loot into a small event: someone has to decide whether the upgraded payout is worth being the one who sets it off.

  • Default cursed drop chance is 0.08% per eligible zombie.
  • Default reward journal skills are 2 to 5 entries worth 75 to 300 XP.
  • Default reward extras are 40% trait chance, 60% recipe chance, and 25% forget-slot chance.

Key sandbox controls

  • Enable Cursed Journal Drops and Cursed Drop Chance determine whether this family appears at all.
  • Disguise Cursed Journals As Bloody lets the journal masquerade as a standard bloody drop until someone breaks the seal.
  • Cursed Min/Max Skills, Cursed Min/Max XP, and the trait/recipe/forget-slot toggles tune the reward journal after the curse resolves.
  • If a cursed drop rolls successfully, the normal bloody drop is skipped for that zombie.

Trivia

Cursed journal notes

  • The context menu text for cursed journals is not just "Open" but "Break the Seal...", which sells the whole idea before the player even clicks it.
  • The panic curse is more than a flavor text event: it has its own spawn ring, AI pull pulses, and delayed zombie nudges layered on top of the initial sound burst.
  • One of the explicit curse flavors is a seasonal heat or cold spike, tying cursed journals back into BSJ's broader event vocabulary instead of limiting them to damage-only punishments.
