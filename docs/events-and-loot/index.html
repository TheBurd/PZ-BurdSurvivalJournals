How journals enter the world

The current build spreads BSJ content across container loot, zombie drops, fun-journal rolls, and seasonal replacements.

Container loot Zombie loot Cursed reveals Cursed ambush profile Yuletide season Yuletide gift tiers

Container loot: worn journals

  • Worn journals are the standard world-loot branch.
  • The server controls whether they spawn at all and how strong they can be.
  • During Yuletide season, some eligible worn rolls can be replaced by wrapped Yuletide journals.

Zombie loot: bloody and cursed journals

  • Bloody journals are the baseline zombie-drop branch.
  • Cursed journals sit above that as a fun-loot branch and can interrupt a normal bloody result if they roll first.
  • During Yuletide season, eligible bloody drops can be replaced by Yuletide journals after cursed precedence is checked.

Cursed journal reveals

Before the reveal

The journal may appear obviously cursed or be disguised as a normal bloody journal if the disguise toggle is enabled.

After the reveal

The opener takes one live curse effect, then the journal converts into a stronger unleashed reward journal.

Curse lane Live behavior
Direct injury Barbed seal targets a random hand; jammed breath crushes endurance and adds panic plus stress.
Gear sabotage Hexed tooling damages one eligible weapon or tool; torn gear adds 3 to 5 fresh clothing tears; pantsed removes qualifying lower-body clothing.
Character punishment The opener can gain a negative trait, lose a current positive trait, or lose 1 current skill level.
Environmental hit Seasonal wave turns into a heat spike or cold snap depending on the current world month.
Ambush event Panic sets panic to 100 and attempts an 8 to 12 zombie spawn around the opener.
Player takeaway: if a server enables cursed journals, zombie loot can occasionally be a gamble instead of a straight upgrade.

Ambush count The panic roll attempts 8 to 12 zombies.

Yuletide season behavior

  • Season activation can follow the save's in-game date, the host's real-world date, or both.
  • By default, world date is on and host real date is off.
  • Eligible worn journals have a default 4.0% replacement chance; eligible bloody drops have a default 8.0% replacement chance after cursed precedence is checked.
  • Yuletide journals can include skills, traits, recipes, forget slots, and physical gift items.
  • If enabled, active Yuletide seasons also swap newly spawned cursed journal authors over to Krampus.
